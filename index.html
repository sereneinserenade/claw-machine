How to play

  • Joystick or arrow keys move the claw
  • DROP or Space drops it
  • 2×Tap DROP again to grab early
  • Tap the door to take your prize
  • ★One prize glows gold each night. Win it for a star on your shelf
  • 3×Win 3 of the same prize and it turns shiny

Grip modes

Arcade grip fades as the claw rises, like a real machine. The better you center it, the more often it holds, and every few plays it stays strong all the way. The pink bar under the sign fills as that play gets closer.

Fair grip holds whenever you center the claw over a prize. Prizes you nudge into the chute count too.
